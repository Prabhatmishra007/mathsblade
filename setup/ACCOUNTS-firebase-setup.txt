MathsBlade – turn on real student accounts (Firebase, free, ~10 minutes)
=======================================================================

Until you do this, Sign up / Sign in runs in DEMO MODE: accounts are saved
only in that student's browser. With Firebase, students get real accounts
that work on any phone or computer, plus "Continue with Google" and
"Forgot password" emails.

1. Go to https://console.firebase.google.com and click "Create a project".
   Name it "mathsblade". Google Analytics is optional.

2. In the project, open Build > Authentication > Get started.
   On the "Sign-in method" tab, enable:
   - Email/Password
   - Google (choose your support email)

3. Still in Authentication, open Settings > Authorized domains and add
   your website's domain (for example mathsblade.in or yoursite.netlify.app).

4. Click the gear icon > Project settings > General. Under "Your apps",
   click the web icon (</>), register an app named "MathsBlade web".
   Firebase shows a firebaseConfig object like:

     const firebaseConfig = {
       apiKey: "AIza…",
       authDomain: "mathsblade.firebaseapp.com",
       projectId: "mathsblade",
       appId: "1:…:web:…"
     };

5. Open js/config.js, find this line:
       const FIREBASE_CONFIG = null;
   and replace null with the object, for example:
       const FIREBASE_CONFIG = {apiKey:"AIza…",authDomain:"mathsblade.firebaseapp.com",projectId:"mathsblade",appId:"1:…:web:…"};
   Save and upload the site. (This config is meant to be public; it is safe
   to put in the page.)

6. Optional: Authentication > Templates lets you brand the password-reset
   email with the MathsBlade name.

Where student details go
- Name, email and password: Firebase Authentication (you can see users in
  the Firebase console > Authentication > Users).
- School and phone: sent to your Google Sheet ("Registrations" tab) if you
  set up the leaderboard sheet (README.txt), and saved on the student's device.

Note: real accounts and the shared leaderboard only work on your own
website. On the claude.ai preview link, students sign in with their Claude
account instead.
