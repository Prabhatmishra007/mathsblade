MathsBlade – connect the shared leaderboard (about 5 minutes)
=============================================================

Without this step, the leaderboard on your own website only shows scores
saved on each student's own device. Follow these steps once to share it.

1. Open Google Sheets and create a new, blank spreadsheet.
   Name it something like "MathsBlade Board Exam".

2. In the sheet, click Extensions > Apps Script.

3. Delete the sample code and paste everything from
   leaderboard-apps-script.gs. Click Save.

4. Click Deploy > New deployment.
   - Select type: Web app
   - Execute as: Me
   - Who has access: Anyone
   Click Deploy and allow the permissions Google asks for.

5. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec).

6. Open js/config.js in a text editor, find this line:
       const LEADERBOARD_SHEET_URL = "";
   and paste your URL between the quotes. Save and upload the site.

What you get
- "Registrations" sheet: every student who starts a paper (name, school, email, phone).
- "Scores" sheet: every submitted score with full contact details.
- The public leaderboard shows only first name + surname initial, school and scores.
  Emails and phone numbers never leave your sheet.
- To mark Sections B–E yourself, type a total in the "Teacher total"
  column. The leaderboard will use it and label the row "teacher-checked".

If you edit the script later: Deploy > Manage deployments > Edit > New version.

Privacy note: students may be under 18. Keep the sheet private and use the
contact details only for MathsBlade results and updates.
